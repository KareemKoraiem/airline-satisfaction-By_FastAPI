About

About This Project

This tool uses a Machine Learning classification model trained on real airline passenger survey data to predict whether a passenger will be satisfied or dissatisfied with their flight experience. It considers 14 service dimensions including seat comfort, in-flight entertainment, food quality, WiFi, and more.
